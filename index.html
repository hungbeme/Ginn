GiNN (Gen Z Involvement in a New Nigeria)

GiNN (Gen Z Involvement in a New Nigeria) is a youth-driven movement focused on leadership, innovation, civic awareness, and nation-building. It exists to inspire young Nigerians to move from conversation to action.

Founded on the belief that Nigeria’s greatest asset is its youth, GiNN exists to mobilize, educate, connect, and empower Gen Z Nigerians to actively participate in shaping a progressive, inclusive, and prosperous nation.
We believe the future of Nigeria will be shaped by the decisions of its youth today.
